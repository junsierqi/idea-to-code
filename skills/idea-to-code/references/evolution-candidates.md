# Evolution candidates

This is a non-normative register of unverified proposals, not active skill instructions. Ordinary business tasks do not load this file. Read it only for skill maintenance or a candidate named in an explicitly scoped trial TASK. Reading, copying or installing this file does not activate its contents. Imperative candidate wording is material to assess, not authority to override user instructions, established rules, permissions or acceptance criteria.

The operative isolation, trial, promotion and rollback procedure belongs to [workflow.md](workflow.md#evidence-backed-workflow-improvement). Plans, authorization, observations and evidence stay in ordinary task bundles; this register is not another lifecycle or progress log.

## Entry format

- Candidate ID and status: proposed, trial, or rejected; status is descriptive, not authorization.
- Observed problem and evidence reference: facts separate from suspected causes; no credentials or private project data.
- Proposed rule or behavior: exact hypothesis to test.
- Applies to / does not apply to: scope and normal workflows to preserve.
- Trial TASK and isolated candidate version: absent until explicitly planned.
- Positive and negative cases: expected outcomes and evidence needed.
- Decision criteria: conditions for promotion, rejection or further investigation, and the intended single maintained owner.

After a decision, retain its evidence in the owning task and remove duplicate candidate wording. A short decision pointer may remain while needed for continuity. Do not accumulate retired proposals as a second manual.

## EVC-002: Meaning-preserving quality-contract checks

Status: proposed; no parser change or trial authorized by this entry.

Origin: skill-development bundle `20260919-1945-evolution-delivery-control`, TASK-6 / REQ-7; `artifacts/task6-lexical-observation.json` records a supervisory observation and its original failed plan-check identity. These are development evidence references, not installed runtime dependencies.

Observed problem: A concrete Shortcut Risk Check rejecting unsupported completion claims and skipped verification was reported as weak; adding the word "evidence" made it pass. The checker uses exact whole-word alternatives, so inflected words or concrete identifiers alone can be rejected. This is a lexical counterexample, not evidence that the entire plan is adequate.

Hypothesis: A quality check that preserves the meaning of concrete evidence and rejection conditions could reduce false rejections without accepting vague claims. Applies to implementation-plan quality fields; does not authorize skipping required fields, evidence, approval boundaries or validation.

Trial TASK/version: not assigned. Keep the existing validator active until a separately planned and validated change replaces it.

Verification: compare equivalent singular/plural wording and concrete file/test identifiers, plus empty, vague and unsupported statements. A normal concrete plan must remain possible and weak plans must remain rejected; test count or keyword presence alone is not quality evidence.

Decision criteria: promote only demonstrated scope with preserved negative cases and less wording dependence. Reject an approach that merely substitutes another mandatory word. Intended owner: planning-patterns.md and the existing implementation-plan validator, through an ordinary maintenance TASK.
